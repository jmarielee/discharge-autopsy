PRIMARY DEFECT     THRESHOLD_ABSENCE
TIER               P2
LOCUS              observability

ANCHORS            specimen-01, Assessment and Plan:
                   "okay to return to activity & guide themselves based on pain"

                   specimen-01, Assessment and Plan:
                   "motrin or tylenol if needed for pain control, no narcotics
                   necessary"

CHAIN              Two instructions to act are each conditioned on a criterion the
                   artifact set never quantifies.

                   The first makes pain the governing bound on activity. No lifting
                   limit, no weight, no duration, no activity class, and no
                   restriction end date appears anywhere in the document. The
                   reader is directed to a self-administered test and given no
                   scale on which to read its result.

                   The second conditions analgesic use on "if needed" and states
                   two agents with no amount, no interval, and no daily maximum.
                   The Current Medications table carries columns for Name,
                   Prescribed Date, and Start Date; no dose, route, or frequency
                   column exists in the document, so the amount is not recoverable
                   from elsewhere in the set.

                   Both bounds are absent in the same direction. A caregiver
                   reading this at home, without the encounter, has two instructions
                   to act and no stated point at which either instruction stops.

RULED OUT          ACTION_DIVERGENCE (P1). Not present in this set. The seroma
                   counsel — "counseled regarding seroma formation, recommended
                   warm compresses/heating pad & compression, may take up to 90 days
                   to resolve" — assigns one action to one observable finding, and
                   no second instruction in this document assigns an opposing action
                   to the same finding. The divergence requires a second document.
                   Eliminated on set contents, not on strength.

                   STATE_MISMATCH (P3). Every content span is scoped to the
                   documented state. The narrative reads "Patient presents s/p
                   robotic right inguinal hernia repair with mesh", the Problems
                   table carries "Right Inguinal Hernia | Active", and the Reason
                   for Visit reads "Post Operative Visit". No span is scoped to a
                   state other than the one recorded. Eliminated on absence of a
                   conflicting scope statement.

WOULD FLIP THIS    Any bound on either instruction, anywhere in the document — a
                   weight, a duration, a restriction end date, an activity class,
                   a dose, an interval, or a daily maximum. If such a span exists
                   and was missed, this verdict fails. It would also flip on the
                   arrival of a second document in the set: a companion handout
                   assigning an opposing action to an observable finding named here
                   would introduce a P1 and outrank this.

FILED AS SYMPTOM   STRUCTURED_NARRATIVE_CONTRADICTION (P4), two instances. The
                   narrative reads "follow up at 1 year post op, call with any
                   questions/issues" while the Plan of Care table records
                   "None recorded." at the Appointments row. Separately, the
                   narrative records counseling delivered at the visit while the
                   structured field reads "Discussion Note: None recorded."
                   Demoted: outranked by P2.

                   CONTACT_ABSENCE (P5). Three contact directives — "please contact
                   our office", "call with any questions/issues", and "Contact your
                   provider if this list needs to be updated" — with no phone
                   number, portal instruction, or after-hours guidance present in
                   the document. Demoted, and flagged pending verification: the
                   letterhead and the Demographics contact field are redacted in the
                   repository copy. This finding may be an artifact of redaction
                   rather than a property of the original and cannot be resolved
                   against this copy.

                   FIELD_INCOMPLETENESS (P6). "Patient educational handouts: No
                   information available." Also the Prescribed Date and Start Date
                   columns, present and empty across all nine medication rows.
                   Demoted: tier P6.

RESOLUTION PATH    Bring this document to the discharging clinician or a pharmacist.
